PerUnit Grocery Compare compares two products by price per unit (per kg, per liter, or per item) so you can see which one is the better deal.

How to use

  1. Enter the price and quantity of item A and item B, and pick the unit.
  2. Tap Compare Unit Prices.
  3. The app shows each price per unit and how much cheaper the winner is.
